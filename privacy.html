Privacy policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Shakedown ("the app") helps people split shared expenses. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Google account info. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile photo. We use them to identify you to the other members of your groups.
  • Content you add. Groups, trips, expenses, splits, settlements, notes, and the receipt or cover photos you choose to attach.
  • Contacts you pick. If you invite people from your contacts, the app reads your contacts on your device so you can choose people. Only the email address of each person you choose to invite is sent to our server, to create the invitation. We do not upload your address book.
  • Photos and camera. Used only when you attach a receipt or cover image. Only the images you select are uploaded.

How we use it

To run the app: show you and your group members the shared expenses and balances, deliver invitations, and keep your account signed in. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

Who can see your data

Other members of a group you belong to can see that group's expenses, balances, receipts, and each member's name and email. Nobody outside your groups can see them.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and sign-in is handled by Google. Both process data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can leave any group from within the app. To have your account and data deleted, contact us and we will remove it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Contact

Open an issue with any privacy questions.
