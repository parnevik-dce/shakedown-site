Shakedown

Track what's owed. Nobody skips out.

Shakedown is an iPhone app for splitting shared expenses with roommates, trips, couples and friend groups. Log who paid, split it however you like, and always know who owes whom.

What it does

  • Create groups and trips, and invite people by link, code, or from your contacts
  • Split expenses equally, by exact amounts, or by percentage
  • Attach a receipt photo to any expense
  • See running balances and simplified debts that minimize the number of payments
  • Record settle-ups and keep a history of everything in the group

Shakedown keeps the books only. It does not move real money.
